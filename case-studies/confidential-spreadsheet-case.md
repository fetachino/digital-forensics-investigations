# Confidential Spreadsheet Case

## Course context

CIT 42000 Digital Forensics — Assignment 5, “You Tell Me.”

## Approach

Preserved the supplied forensic images, correlated filesystem and user-activity artifacts, compared timestamps, reconstructed document movement, and separated observations from attribution.

## Takeaway

The exercise demonstrates evidence handling, timeline reasoning, and cautious reporting in a data-leak investigation.

> Sanitization: credentials, forensic images, personal identifiers, and exact evidence values are not published.
