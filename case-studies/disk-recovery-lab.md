# Disk-recovery lab: TestDisk and Sleuth Kit

## Scope

This sanitized case study covers controlled recovery of deleted files and filesystem metadata with TestDisk and Sleuth Kit. Original disk images, recovered files, and case identifiers are not redistributed.

## Methodology

- Worked from a forensic copy rather than an original device.
- Verified the image metadata and recorded tool versions.
- Examined partitions, filesystem structures, deleted entries, and file metadata.
- Documented recovered artifacts with source, confidence, and limitations.

## Findings and lessons

The lab reinforced write-blocking principles, repeatable acquisition, hash-based integrity checks, and the need to distinguish recoverable remnants from validated evidence. Only the workflow and reporting approach are presented here.
