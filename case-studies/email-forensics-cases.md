# Email Forensics Cases

## Course context

CIT 42000 Digital Forensics — Assignment 6, “You Got Mail!”

## Approach

Reviewed email headers, sender/recipient relationships, timestamps, routing, packet-level evidence, attachments, and anomalous behavior. Built timelines, identified key players, and documented the difference between direct evidence and inference.

## Takeaway

This work demonstrates email triage, network-forensics correlation, timeline reconstruction, and clear technical reporting.

> Sanitization: packet captures, email files, external evidence URLs, personal data, and malicious artifacts are not redistributed.
